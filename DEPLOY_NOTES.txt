   - assignApplicationToCounselor
3. Added assignApplicationToCounselor(raw) as a compatibility alias.
4. Kept Master Sheet1 untouched for dashboard-only assignments.
5. Kept Firebase assignment + counselor-sheet routing architecture intact.
