QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before applying.

Who can apply?+

Students from 1st year through final year, as well as eligible passed-out students, may apply across degree and diploma programmes.

What is the programme mode and duration?+

The programme is online and is generally structured across a 2–3 month period. Exact schedules may vary by selected track or batch.

Does submitting guarantee selection?+

No. Submission records your interest and allows the programme team to review your eligibility and preferred domain.

Is a stipend guaranteed?+

No. Any stipend opportunity is performance-based and subject to eligibility, applicable work and programme terms.

When is certification provided?+

Certification is subject to completing the applicable programme, project, attendance or assessment requirements communicated during onboarding.

How will my details be used?+

Your details are used for application review, programme communication, onboarding assistance and related internal records as described in the privacy notice.

What happens after I submit?+

You will see a confirmation screen with your application reference. Sharing the opportunity with classmates is optional.
