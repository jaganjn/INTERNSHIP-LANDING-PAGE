Privacy Notice

Effective: August 3, 2026

This notice describes the data handled by the InternsForge internship landing page. Update the organisation’s official contact information before public deployment when a dedicated privacy contact is available.

Information collected

The application collects your name, WhatsApp number, email address, college, department, current year, selected domain, referral information and consent choices. The page may also record limited technical and progress information, such as application step, completion percentage and submission time.

How information is used

  • Reviewing internship programme applications.
  • Communicating application, eligibility, onboarding and programme updates.
  • Maintaining internal programme and referral records.
  • Understanding where students abandon or complete the form so the experience can be improved.

Draft saving

An unfinished application draft may be stored locally in your browser for up to 14 days. It remains on that device and can be removed using the “Start again” control.

Sharing and service providers

Application records may be stored using Firebase and copied to an authorised Google Sheet workflow used by the programme team. Personal information should not be sold or used for unrelated advertising. Optional referral messages do not include the applicant’s personal details.

Data choices

The communication checkbox is optional. The terms and privacy confirmation is required to submit. Applicants may request correction or deletion through the official programme contact shared in verified InternsForge communications.

Security and retention

Reasonable access controls should be maintained for databases and administrative tools. Information should be retained only for legitimate application, programme, compliance and support needs, then deleted or anonymised when no longer required.

Important note

Do not submit passwords, banking PINs, government identity numbers or payment card details through this application form.
