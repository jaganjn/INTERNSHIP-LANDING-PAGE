Programme Terms

Effective: August 3, 2026

These are clear landing-page terms, not a substitute for programme-specific onboarding documents. Final schedules, fees, deliverables and policies should be provided before enrolment or payment.

Application and selection

Submitting the form records an applicant’s interest. It does not guarantee selection, enrolment, internship placement, employment, stipend, scholarship or any other outcome.

Eligibility and information

Applicants must provide accurate information and use contact details they control. The programme team may review eligibility, available tracks, batch capacity and other relevant requirements.

Programme format

The programme is described as online and generally structured over 2–3 months. Exact dates, live-session schedules, project requirements and weekly effort may vary by track or batch and should be confirmed during onboarding.

Projects and certification

Project scope depends on the selected domain and student level. Any certificate or completion credential is subject to the applicable attendance, assessment, project, conduct and completion requirements communicated for the programme.

Stipend and placement

Stipend opportunities, where offered, are performance-based and subject to separate eligibility and terms. Placement preparation or assistance does not guarantee a job, interview or offer.

Technology brands

Third-party company names, logos, platforms or tools may describe ecosystems used in selected tracks. Their appearance does not by itself mean that those companies sponsor, employ, certify or partner with every programme offering.

Communication

Applicants who opt in may receive relevant updates through WhatsApp, phone or email. Applicants should verify that communications come from an authorised programme channel before making payments or sharing sensitive information.

Referral sharing

Sharing after submission is optional and does not affect the applicant’s own review. Referral eligibility or rewards, where applicable, require separate verification and terms.

Changes

Programme details may be updated for legitimate operational reasons. Material details should be communicated before an applicant commits to enrolment or payment.
